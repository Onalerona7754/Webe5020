About Hope Haven

Our History

Founded in 2018, Hope Haven is a non-profit shelter dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming abandoned and stray domestic animals within the community. Over the past 8 years, our organization has rescued over 1,200 animals.


Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide medical care, temporary shelter, and loving homes for stray and abandoned animals while educating the public on responsible pet ownership.

Vision: A community where every domestic animal is treated with compassion and has a safe home.
